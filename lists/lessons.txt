Grammar topic: so (that) ... doesn't — purpose clauses.
Level A2. How to say why someone does something, when the purpose is to avoid
something: "She peels off the wrappers slowly so the crunch doesn't give her away."
Cover: so + subject + verb, so that + subject + verb, the negative form, present
and past, the difference from "because" (reason vs purpose), and the mistake
Ukrainian speakers make by translating "щоб не" with "for not".

Grammar topic: remember / see / hear / watch + object + -ing.
Level A2-B1. "I remember him saying that", not "I remember him to say that".
Cover: which verbs take object + -ing; the difference between "remember doing"
(a memory of the past) and "remember to do" (not forgetting a task); why the
infinitive is wrong after these verbs.

Grammar topic: there is / there are + something + -ing.
Level A2. "There's something going on", "There were people waiting outside".
Cover: the structure there + be + noun + -ing, singular and plural, present and
past, negatives and questions, and how it differs from the plain present
continuous ("Something is going on").

Grammar topic: the fact that — turning a whole sentence into a noun.
Level B1. "The fact that nobody noticed surprised her."
Cover: the fact that + subject + verb as the subject or object of a sentence;
when it is needed and when "that" alone is enough; common wordiness.

Grammar topic: type of / kind of / sort of + noun.
Level A2. "These types of errands", "this kind of problem".
Cover: agreement between this/that/these/those, type/types and the noun that
follows; singular and plural; when the noun takes an article and when it does not.
